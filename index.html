Good to know

Anything else, just ask.

07781 135100
Can you take a wheelchair?

Yes. Our Peugeot Horizon carries a small to medium-sized wheelchair and one additional passenger. If it’s already booked, we may be able to arrange your journey through one of our partner accessible-vehicle drivers.

How do I book?

Call, text or WhatsApp 07781 135100, email premiercarslimited@gmail.com, or use the form below. Booking ahead is best, especially for early mornings and specialist transfers.

How many passengers can you take?

Up to six in one booking. For larger groups, just ask.

Are port transfers a fixed price?

Yes. Port transfers are fixed price, and you can pay for them in advance.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, we’re dog friendly. Please mention it when you book.

When are you available?

Seven days a week, day and night, including bank holidays.
