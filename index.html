Accessible taxis and patient drivers

For anyone who needs a little more help, physical or cognitive. Every driver is vetted, government licensed and trained by us.

Wheelchair accessible

Our accessible vehicle is a Peugeot Horizon. It carries a small to medium-sized wheelchair and one more passenger. We work closely with other WAV drivers, so if ours is booked we may still be able to help through one of our partners.

Please give us as much notice as you can for specialist transfers.
