Good to know

Anything else, just ask.

Can you take a wheelchair?

Yes. Our vehicle is a Peugeot Horizon, which carries a small to medium-sized wheelchair and one additional passenger. We work closely with other WAV drivers, so if ours isn’t the right fit we may be able to help through one of our partners.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, we’re dog friendly – assistance dogs and pets alike. Just mention it when you book.

Can I book by text or WhatsApp?

Yes. Call, text or WhatsApp 07781 135100, or email premiercarslimited@gmail.com.

Do you do early-morning pick-ups?

Yes, including early flights and boats. Book your transfer early to avoid disappointment.

Can I pay for a port transfer in advance?

Yes. Port transfers are available at a fixed price, and the fare can be paid in advance.
